## Handoff and control

For B/C, three consecutive observations with actual confirmed grasp, no major transform drift, bowl XY within 9 cm of physical plate center, and bowl center at least 2.5 cm above the calculated support target enter PLACE/ALIGN. Handoff is latched; it does not wait for the human reference endpoint. The current feasible gripper-to-bowl transform is captured. On the old trace the first eligible sample is step 89, so takeover is deliberately earlier than the step-211 opening. This is an offline eligibility check, not a prediction of new closed-loop behavior.

ALIGN moves bowl center over plate while preserving handoff height, using shortest world-frame opening-axis-to-gravity corrections with no yaw objective. Three low-speed observations within 12 mm horizontally and 8 degrees tilt enter LOWER. LOWER targets the physical support height. The target is the central plate collision-box top minus the minimum upright bowl collision-box bottom; it ignores visual extents. The 12 mm centering tolerance is a conservative center-target tolerance, not a claim about liquid physics or full-footprint containment. Actual bowl?plate collision contact, 4 mm height tolerance, <=0.025 m/s speed, <=8 degree tilt, confirmed grasp, and no major drift must hold for three observations before RELEASE. Contact is a boolean geometry diagnostic, not a load/force estimate.

Each command uses the existing affine world-relative OSC scaling, bounds the commanded EEF position increment to 3 mm and orientation increment to 0.02 rad. These are command limits, not guarantees about realized acceleration. Gripper is explicitly closed through TRANSPORT and PLACE in B and C, then explicitly opened through RELEASE/VERIFY; policy cannot re-close it. Human guidance and raw policy motor actions are ignored in placement. RELEASE holds the EEF pose while opening until aperture increases by 20 mm (capped at 70 mm aperture) and grasp contact clears for three observations. VERIFY withdraws vertically 6 cm, keeps opening, allows at least ten steps, and checks the unchanged LIBERO predicate. Timeouts: ALIGN 65, LOWER 65, RELEASE 25, VERIFY 35 steps, within the unchanged official 280-step horizon. Failure preserves logs.

The official evaluator terminates immediately on success. For B/C placement only, the wrapper defers success termination until withdrawal/verification completes; it keeps the original predicate and info is_success visible. Summary records any observed LIBERO success, final predicate, and controller completion separately. A termination and actions remain unchanged. The evaluator's aggregate success means success seen at any step; a later placement failure is still reported separately.
